ACPFedRec
------
A federated recommendation framework combining metadata-based preference clustering, centroid smoothing, and activity-conditioned inference. Evaluated on four recommendation datasets; accepted at WSDM 2027.

[Read the paper]({{ '/files/ACPFedRec_WSDM2027.pdf' | relative_url }})

PRE-ACT
------
Contribution to research on federated learning and explainability in the Horizon Europe PRE-ACT project at the Athens University of Economics and Business.

Quality-of-life recommendation
------
Ongoing research into personalized recommendation and federated learning for quality-of-life applications, with attention to explainability and differences between user groups.
