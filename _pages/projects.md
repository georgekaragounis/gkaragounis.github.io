ACPFedRec
------
**Personalized federated recommendation · Research**

A federated recommendation framework combining metadata-based preference clustering, centroid smoothing, and activity-conditioned inference. Evaluated on four recommendation datasets; accepted as a main-track paper at WSDM 2027.
